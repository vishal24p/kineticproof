# Physics engine — separate measurements from perception

The tracker supplies per-frame target centers and source timestamps, not period or physics truth. The deterministic period engine projects each valid center onto a normalized user-supplied 2D motion axis: `x * axis.x + y * axis.y`. Frames without finite centers or flagged `tracking_failed` split the trace into contiguous segments. The engine reports those gap ranges, adds a missing-data warning even when measurement succeeds, and never forms a cycle across a gap.

Within each segment, interior samples are filtered with a three-sample median (previous, current, next); the segment endpoints remain unchanged. Candidate local maxima, including plateaus represented by their midpoint sample, are evaluated using topographic prominence: scan each side up to a strictly higher sample or the segment boundary, then subtract the higher of those two side minima from the peak height. Equal-height peaks do not stop the scan. Each segment's acceptance threshold is 10% of that segment's filtered signal range. The result reports every segment threshold and the aggregate `prominence_threshold_px` is their maximum. Candidate maxima below their segment threshold are counted as rejected. Consecutive accepted maxima in a segment define same-direction cycles, timed using source PTS. The JSON `measurement_config` discloses `filter_name: "median"`, `filter_window_samples: 3`, `detector: "local_maximum"`, `prominence_method: "topographic"`, and `prominence_fraction: 0.1`; the tracking provenance's `run_config_id` identifies the tracker configuration separately.

The reported period is the median of detected cycle periods only when at least two cycles are available. `cycle_mad_seconds` is the median absolute deviation (MAD): the median of absolute differences between each cycle period and that median. `source_timing_resolution_seconds` is the median of positive adjacent timestamp differences across the trace. These are descriptive measures, not confidence intervals, and no confidence-interval claim is made. Fewer than two detected cycles produces `insufficient_data`, with no measured period; CSV remains header-only even when one cycle exists.

Optional manual reference events may be supplied as `leftmost`, `rightmost`, or `same_direction` events with timestamp, evidence frame index, and timing uncertainty. Repeated events of the same type form intervals; the reported manual reference period is the median of those intervals. These are independent annotations only when a person actually produced and reviewed them; the API does not verify their quality. Optional theoretical period is calculated only when both positive finite effective length `L` and gravity `g` are provided: `T_theory = 2π sqrt(L/g)`. Arithmetic evaluates `(2π sqrt(L)) / sqrt(g)` to avoid overflow or underflow in the intermediate ratio. A derived period that is not finite and positive returns HTTP 422 for either response format. It depends on the simple-pendulum and small-angle assumptions, with length measured from pivot to center of mass, and is not independently observed ground truth.

Pixel coordinates do not justify metric speed or true 3D acceleration; metric motion requires scale and camera geometry calibration. Authentic-clip validity remains unverified. Synthetic tests establish implementation behavior only, not real-world tracking or measurement accuracy. Do not let a language model fill measurement fields.
